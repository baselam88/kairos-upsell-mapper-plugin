# Classic Upsell Mapper

Maps the upsell offered at maximum buying energy: the next problem the purchase just created — more of it, a better version, or the missing piece.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Highest-gross-profit upsell first; guarantees are sold as upsells, not given away. The three killers void the attempt: wrong thing, wrong time, unbelieved.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Classic Upsell Mapper to help me: maps the upsell offered at maximum buying energy: the next problem the purchase just created — more of it, a better version, or the missing piece — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
